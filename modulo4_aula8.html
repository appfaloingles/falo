Comment on the past — comente o passado

Critique, imagine e deduza o passado. Em voz alta.

1
Faça uma crítica: "You should have called me."
2
Fale de uma chance perdida: "I could have won."
3
Deduza o passado: "She must have forgotten."
4
Use o negativo: "You shouldn't have said that."
