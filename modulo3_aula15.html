Connect your ideas — conecte ideias

Junte duas frases numa só. Fale em voz alta.

1
Dê uma causa: "I'm happy because it's Friday."
2
Dê um resultado: "It was raining, so I stayed home."
3
Faça contraste: "I'm tired, but I'll go out."
4
Use 'although': "Although I'm busy, I'll help you."
