BPassado do to be

was / were

Toque pra ouvir. Repare em quem usa WAS e quem usa WERE.

🧠

O passado do 'to be' tem só 2 formas: WAS (I, he, she, it) e WERE (you, we, they). "I WAS happy", "They WERE here". Negativo: wasn't / weren't. É a base pra contar tudo o que já aconteceu!
