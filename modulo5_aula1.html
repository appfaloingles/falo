★Dinâmica do dia

Talk about hypotheticals — fale de hipóteses

Conecte passado e presente. Em voz alta.

1
Passado→presente: "If I had learned to code, I would have a better job."
2
Presente→passado: "If I were braver, I would have asked her out."
3
Use arrependimento: "If only I had started earlier."
4
Fale de você: "If I had studied English before, I'd be fluent now."
