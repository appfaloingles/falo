Add emphasis — dê ênfase

Comece pelo advérbio negativo e inverta. Em voz alta.

1
Com never: "Never have I felt so proud."
2
Com not only: "Not only did she win, but she also broke the record."
3
Com no sooner: "No sooner had I left than they called."
4
Fale de você: "Rarely do I give up on anything."
